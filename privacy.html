1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Streak (“the app”, “we”) processes your personal data. Streak is available for iOS and Android and follows a strict data minimization principle: the app uses no advertising and integrates no third-party analytics services, advertising or tracking SDKs. Your workout data either stays on your device or, on iOS, is stored in your private iCloud account, which only you can access. Apart from your optional iCloud sync, the only data sent to a server operated by us is optional usage statistics, without your name, workout details or health data, which you switch on yourself (see section 2.8). On Android, data stays exclusively on your device unless you choose to export it.

This policy is intended to meet the requirements of the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (DSG/revFADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Data We Process

2.1 Workout & Activity Logs

What: Your workout entries (date, activity type, duration, intensity, notes) and any custom sport types or categories you create.

Where stored:

  • iOS: On your device and, if iCloud is enabled, in your private iCloud CloudKit database. Data is encrypted by Apple and is accessible only to you – the developer has no access.
  • Android: Exclusively on your device in the app’s private storage (Room database). No cloud sync. You can export your data at any time via Settings > Data.

Legal basis (where GDPR applies): Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Retention: Until you delete the data in the app, uninstall the app, or remove it from your iCloud account (iOS). You fully control retention.

2.2 Health Data (Apple Health / Health Connect) Sensitive

What: With your explicit permission, the app integrates with the platform health service:

  • iOS — Apple HealthKit:
    • Read: Workouts (HKWorkoutType), Active Energy Burned, Heart Rate, Resting Heart Rate, Sleep Analysis, Step Count, Body Mass – to import your Apple Health workouts with their energy and average heart rate, to show your daily context (sleep, move, resting heart rate), to derive a workout's intensity, and to estimate calories for the Move ring. Heart-rate samples of a workout are read on demand for the Pro heart-rate curve and are not stored.
    • Write: Workouts (HKWorkoutType) and Active Energy Burned – new workouts can optionally be written back to Apple Health.
  • Android — Health Connect:
    • Read: Exercise Sessions – to import existing workouts from Health Connect into Streak. Writing is currently not implemented on Android.

Where stored: Health data is exchanged directly with Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android) on your device. Raw health data, including heart-rate samples, stays in Apple Health or Health Connect and is never transmitted to the developer or shared with any third party. Values imported into a workout (duration, active energy, average heart rate) become part of that Streak entry and are stored like your other workouts: on your device and, on iOS with iCloud enabled, in your private iCloud database (section 2.1). No health data ever reaches a server operated by the developer. Workouts written to Apple Health are stored and managed by Apple Health and are subject to Apple’s privacy policy.

Sensitive data: Health data is a special category of personal data under Art. 9 GDPR and particularly sensitive personal data under Swiss law. It is processed solely on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you grant when you enable Health integration in the app or in your platform’s health settings.

Withdraw consent:

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > Streak.
  • Android: Open the Health Connect app > App permissions > Streak > revoke access.

Revoking access stops both reading and (on iOS) writing. Workouts already written to Apple Health can be deleted in the Apple Health app.

Streak does not use health data for advertising purposes and does not sell it to third parties or data brokers.

2.3 App Settings & Preferences

What: Your optional name, weekly goal, appearance settings, notification preferences, accent color, and custom background selection.

Where stored: Locally on your device only. iOS uses UserDefaults; Android uses Jetpack DataStore. This data is never transmitted or synced by the app to any server.

Legal basis (where GDPR applies): Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Retention: Until you uninstall the app or reset the settings.

2.4 Widget Data

What: Active days this week, your current streak, and the selected accent colour – used to power the home screen widget.

Where stored: Locally on your device. iOS uses an app group shared container between the app and the widget extension; Android uses the same DataStore the main app reads from, accessed by the Glance widget worker. This data is never transmitted.

Legal basis (where GDPR applies): Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

2.5 In‑App Purchase

Streak Pro is sold exclusively through the platform store:

  • iOS: Apple’s App Store via the StoreKit 2 framework.
  • Android: Google Play via the Google Play Billing Library.

Purchase processing and receipt verification are handled entirely by Apple or Google on your device and through their servers. The app does not use any third-party purchase verification service. The developer has no access to payment details such as credit card numbers.

Legal basis (where GDPR applies): Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

For information about how the platforms process transaction data, see Apple’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

2.6 Local Notifications

Local notifications (for example, daily reminders) are scheduled and delivered entirely by your operating system (iOS or Android). No separate notification server is used and no notification data leaves your device.

2.7 Trial Period Tracking

What: The timestamp of your first app launch, used to determine whether your 7-day free trial is still active.

Where stored: Locally in UserDefaults (iOS) or DataStore (Android). On iOS, it is also mirrored to NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore (iCloud Key-Value Store) to prevent trial resets through reinstall. No personal identification is associated with this timestamp.

Legal basis (where GDPR applies): Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

2.8 Optional Usage Statistics opt-in

If, and only if, you switch on “Send usage statistics” (during onboarding or later in Settings), the app sends a small number of events, without your name, workout details or health data, to a server operated by the developer and hosted in Switzerland. The switch is off by default.

What is sent: which screen or action occurred (for example “app opened”, “workout logged”, “purchase screen shown”, “purchase completed”), the app version, iOS version, device model and language, and a random installation identifier that the app generates itself and replaces with a new one every 45 days, to limit how long usage events can be linked to one installation. For a logged workout, only the sport category is included.

What is never sent: your name, e-mail address, Apple ID, exercises, weights, notes, durations, health data, location, advertising identifiers, or any account identifier or directly identifying information such as your name, e-mail address or Apple ID.

IP address: your IP address reaches the server as part of every internet connection. The analytics software uses it only in memory, to derive the country and region and to compute a daily-rotating, non-reversible hash that separates one device from another for that single day. The IP address itself is not written to the database or to any log, and the key for the daily hash is deleted after two days, so the IP-derived hash itself cannot be used to link activity across days. The separate installation identifier described above links opted-in events for up to 45 days.

Legal basis and withdrawal: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; Art. 31(1) Swiss FADP). You can withdraw it at any time by switching the option off in Settings. The installation identifier is then deleted on your device, so later events can no longer be connected to earlier ones. To have the events already stored under your identifier deleted, e-mail the identifier (shown in Settings while the option is on) to hello@tekneio.com; we delete them within 30 days. Identifiers that have already been replaced by the 45-day rotation can no longer be attributed to you, so events stored under them can neither be exported nor deleted individually; they expire with the retention period below.

Retention: raw events are deleted after 365 days; aggregated figures (for example “number of app starts per week”) contain no identifier and are kept for as long as needed to develop the app.

No third-party analytics provider: the analytics software is self-hosted on hardware operated by the developer in Switzerland; data is transmitted TLS-encrypted, and no third-party analytics provider receives it.

6. Security
